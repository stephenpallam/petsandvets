<product_requirements>
The AI Pets and Vets application aims to automate content for Timesheet, Social Media, and Email AI agents. The core requirement was to introduce an **SMS Agent Type** with Scheduled (holiday-based), Recurring (topic-based), and Write Your SMS modes. This agent needed to support short SMS templates with customer placeholders, integrate Twilio (primary sender) and SendGrid, and leverage existing customer phone numbers with ChatGPT for content generation.

UI/UX requirements included Send Immediately options, clear labels, single-customer search, checkbox-based SMS type, a combined Scheduled Date and Send Time, and a centralized SMS Configuration page. <current_work>
Immediately prior to this summary, the AI engineer was addressing a user-reported issue concerning **duplicate subject lines** in email agent posts, specifically for holiday and recurring email agents. The user observed that the Email Subject field (from user input) and a Subject: line embedded within the email content itself were both present in generated posts. The user sought confirmation that only the explicit Email Subject would be used for sending, and the duplicate one in the content would be ignored or not generated.

The engineer investigated and confirmed that the backend email sending process indeed only utilizes the dedicated Email Subject: field, while any Subject: text within the content is merely redundant visual clutter. To fix this, the ChatGPT formatting prompts for both holiday and recurring email generation functions ( and  in  or  as they pass prompts to LLM) were updated. The modified prompts now explicitly instruct ChatGPT to **exclude any subject line or Subject: text** from the generated email content and to start directly with the greeting. This ensures consistency across all email agent types, providing a clean email content body without duplicate subjects.
