1.  **Configuration Form**: To select content type (Topic, Holidays, Custom Campaign), channels (Social Media, Email, SMS with personalization), schedule, link, image options, and workflow mode. Content generation must be platform-specific via ChatGPT.
2.  **Dashboard Display**: Show scheduled/manual run times and detailed campaign info (content type, channels, personalization, previews).
3.  **Content Generation Logic**: Generate consistent content across channels. For Topic/Holiday, ChatGPT generates 150-200 words. Custom campaigns enhance user content. Social media is not personalized; Email/SMS are personalized using templates and customer data, with email requiring a subject.
4.  **Template Management**: A system to create/manage named Email and SMS templates (with/without placeholders) in new configuration page tabs. Global placeholders (e.g., Website Link) in CMS settings should be usable in templates. Templates must be selectable via dropdowns in relevant content text areas.
5.  **UI/UX Enhancements**: Global placeholder editing should be a modal popup. A full-screen editor (later refined to a themed modal) is needed for email content. Templates and placeholders should be filtered based on personalization settings.
6.  **Functional Enhancements**: Social media posts must be platform-specific (style, length, emojis). Email and SMS posts must correctly replace  and other placeholders, adhere to templates, and have proper email subjects/SMS formatting.
